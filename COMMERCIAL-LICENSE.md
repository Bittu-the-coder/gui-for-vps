# Commercial license

GFV is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only), see [LICENSE](LICENSE). For
most people that is all you need: you can run GFV, read it, change it and share it.

The AGPL has one condition that does not suit every situation. If you modify GFV and let other people interact with
your version over a network, you must offer them the source code of your version under the AGPL as well.

A commercial license is a separate agreement that lifts that condition. It may suit you if you want to:

- build GFV, or parts of it, into a closed-source product;
- offer GFV, or a modified version of it, as a hosted or managed service without publishing your changes;
- distribute it inside a product or appliance without passing on the AGPL's terms; or
- use it under terms your company's legal team prefers to the AGPL.

You do **not** need a commercial license to use GFV to manage your own servers or your clients' servers, including at a
company, as long as you are not redistributing a modified version or offering it to others as a service.

## How to ask

Open a discussion or an issue on the repository, titled "Commercial license", and say what you want to do. Please do not
include private business details in a public post: say that you would like to talk privately and I will reply with a way
to do so.

I decide terms case by case. I do not have a price list yet.
