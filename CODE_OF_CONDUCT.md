# Code of conduct

I want GFV to be a friendly place to learn and to build things, for people of every background and experience level.
Many of the people who will use it are beginners, and I do not want anyone to feel stupid for asking a question.

## What I expect

Be respectful and patient. Assume that the other person means well. Disagree about ideas, not about people. Explain
things kindly, especially to someone who is new. When you give feedback on someone's work, be specific and be
constructive.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing other people's private
information, and deliberately disruptive behaviour are not acceptable anywhere in this project: issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other space where it is represented.

## What happens if there is a problem

I am responsible for keeping this a good place, and I may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests,
or ban people who break these rules. If you see something that is not okay, or it happens to you, please tell me
privately using the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). I will take it seriously and I will keep your
report private.

## Where this comes from

This is a short version in my own words of the ideas behind the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). If you want the longer text, that is a good
place to read it.
